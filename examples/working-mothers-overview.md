# Example: Working Mothers Wellbeing (Team Dua) — All 4 Phases

This example illustrates how one project flows through all 4 phases of the UX Case Study Writer skill. **Team Dua** investigated the wellbeing of working mothers in India.

---

## Phase 1: Discovery Brief

**Topic:** Wellbeing of working women in India, with a focus on work-life balance for new mothers.
**Target user:** Working women in the early stages of motherhood.
**Geography:** India, urban nuclear families.

**Funnel progression:**
- Global: Women's workplace participation worldwide
- National: Indian women face barriers (7 in 10 believe women's salary is secondary)
- Sub-group: New mothers with children under 6 (80% cite childcare as reason for leaving)
- Individual: Shikha Handa, Psychologist & Mother

**Key artifacts produced:**
- Emotional arc: 5-stage descending zigzag (Pregnancy > Maternity Leave > Return to Job > Finding Childcare > Career Challenges)
- HMW: "How might we help working women who are in early stages of motherhood attain a sustainable work-life balance?"
- 3 opportunity areas: Modifying work policies (policy) / Community support (social) / Improving childcare (infrastructure)

---

## Phase 2: Empathy Deepening

**New research added:** Field visits to Anganwadi, 5+ stakeholder interviews, bodystorming sessions.

**Key artifacts produced:**
- Filtration funnel: School > University > Employment > Marriage > Children > Pregnancy
- Stakeholder map: 20+ entities across CORE/INVOLVED/INFORMED rings
- 2 contrasting personas: Chitra Tiwari (Software Engineer, nuclear family, daycare) vs. Rashmi Joshi (Construction Worker, joint family, parents care for kids)
- Problem space mind-map: 6 categories (Occupational, Financial, Mental Health, Child Personal, Physical Health, Transport) with 40+ sub-issues
- 3 invalidated assumptions (e.g., "Daycares hinder development" — reality: good daycares promote socialization)

---

## Phase 3: Problem Convergence

**Team scoring session:** 4 members scored 7 candidate problems on 7 criteria individually.

**Key artifacts produced:**
- Radar chart showing individual scoring polygons
- Rejected: PPD (scope mismatch), Household Chores (low interconnectivity)
- Chosen: Childcare (high interconnectivity, under-addressed, tight user fit)
- Ecosystem map: 6 themes (Care Facilities, Physical Health, Mental Health, Development, Transport, Nutrition) with needs/touchpoints/stakeholders
- 15+ daycare pain points orbiting central icon
- Business viability: Indian childcare market US$3.8B (2022) > US$7.3B (2028), 11.2% CAGR (IMARC)
- 3 sub-HMWs: well-being angle / developmental angle / operational angle
- Need statement: "Employed mothers need a reliable service that prioritises mother-child well-being and nutrition while providing children with consistent interaction & connection so that they can feel safe about their child and thrive in their career."

---

## Phase 4: Solution Design

**Research conducted:** Co-creation with 5 mothers + children, A/B testing with 70 mothers.

**Key artifacts produced:**
- Cause & effect tree: 5 macro causes converging on "Insufficient facilities to raise a child"
- 12+ How-to questions across emotional/operational/cultural/safety angles
- Solution: **Togit (Together in Time)** — intergenerational childcare ecosystem
- Primary stakeholders: Elderly, Mother, Children
- 5 key features: Intergenerational Care, Convenient Location (society-embedded), Space Modularity, Togit App, Onboarding Kit
- 9-step service flow ending with "Development of an inclusive and happy society"
- 7 FAQs with mechanism-explaining answers
- Validation: 74% approved, 77% trust, 68% stress reduction
- Wellbeing framework return: Emotional + Occupational (primary), Social + Physical (secondary)
- 3 scalability axes: surplus resource management / existing daycare upgrade / school-workplace partnerships

---

## What This Example Demonstrates

1. **The double diamond shape:** Phase 1 narrows, Phase 2 expands, Phase 3 narrows again, Phase 4 delivers.
2. **Continuity across phases:** The HMW from Phase 1 flows through all subsequent phases. The personas from Phase 2 are referenced in Phase 4.
3. **Progressive evidence deepening:** Phase 1 uses desk research, Phase 2 adds fieldwork, Phase 3 adds scoring + market data, Phase 4 adds co-creation + A/B testing.
4. **No solution leakage:** Phases 1-3 never mention "Togit" or "intergenerational care." Solutions appear only in Phase 4.
5. **Real research grounding:** Every statistic is sourced, every quote is attributed, every field visit is real.
